## Featured projects

### TREVIAN — custom orthotic insoles from a 3D foot scan
A team project (Apr 2025 – Feb 2026) aiming to let anyone scan their foot at home, in minutes, with no extra hardware or professional help, and get a custom insole model in seconds. Custom insoles are normally expensive and require a specialist with dedicated equipment; TREVIAN automates that step. We presented it as exhibitors at **MVPexperience (Nov 2025)** and received very positive feedback from visitors from the sector.

- **How it works:** the pipeline takes a 3D foot scan (STL / USDZ), extracts the sole surface by height and surface-normal filtering, extrudes it into a solid insole in Blender, and applies a lattice post-process so it can be 3D-printed. Orientation normalization (PCA / ICP) is under research.
- **Stack:** Python, trimesh, Open3D, scikit-learn, Blender (bpy), Jupyter, Google Drive API.
- **Repo:** [matiszpek/TREVIAN](https://github.com/matiszpek/TREVIAN) — contains the generation pipeline; the mobile scanning app is not part of the repository.

### EXODIA — exoplanet detection with ML
Machine-learning models that classify exoplanet candidates using public NASA Kepler / TESS / K2 catalogs. Built in a team of three over a weekend for **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025** ("A World Away: Hunting for Exoplanets with AI"); the project received a recognition.
**Stack:** Python, scikit-learn (Random Forest, Extra Trees, AdaBoost, stacking), pandas, Jupyter.
[matiszpek/Exoplanet-detection-using-AI](https://github.com/matiszpek/Exoplanet-detection-using-AI)

### xG SHA — football analytics from video *(in development)*
A personal learning project: a computer-vision model that detects players and the ball in semi-professional matches to derive team and player statistics (possession, xG, passes, heat maps, speed).

### 223D — hand drawings to 3D models *(Jul–Dec 2024, team project)*
A tool that turns photos of hand-drawn views of an object into a 3D model, combining machine vision to digitize the drawings with a custom reconstruction algorithm (voxels, shaders, 3D algorithms). [matiszpek/Proyecto-4to](https://github.com/matiszpek/Proyecto-4to)

### NeoDrift — RC drift car with programmable LEDs *(2023)*
A radio-controlled drift car covered in individually addressable NeoPixel LEDs, with a custom desktop/web app to design the light pattern in real time. Arduino + nRF24L01 radio, later ESP32 + ESP-NOW; 3D-printed parts.
**Stack:** C++/Arduino, ESP32, Node.js, WebSocket.
[matiszpek/Proyecto3ro](https://github.com/matiszpek/Proyecto3ro)
